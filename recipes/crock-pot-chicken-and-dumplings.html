Crock Pot Chicken And Dumplings

This is an easy crock pot recipe for the cold fall and winter months. It takes four to five hours to cook in the crock pot and doesn't require you to make your dumplings from scratch.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of chicken breast
  • 1.5 - 2 cans Pillsbury Buttermilk Flaky Layers Biscuits
  • 2 cups of chicken broth
    • or two chicken bouillon cubes (which is what I normally use)
  • 1 Can of Cream of Chicken
  • 1 Can of Cream and Mushroom
  • 12 oz of frozen mixed vegetables
  • Oregano
  • Thyme
  • Shredded Bay Leaves
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Optional: Chopped Celery

Preparation

  1. No need to cut chicken
  2. If using chicken bouillon cubes, boil in two cups of water and let the cubs dissolve and cool when done
  3. Chop celery

Cook

  1. Place chicken, chicken broth, cream of chicken, cream of mushroom, and seasoning into the crock pot
  2. Cook for 3-4 hours on 400 degrees Fahrenheit
  3. When the chicken is cooked, shred it
  4. Add vegetables and mix in
  5. Cut each biscuit into quatres
  6. Place the quartered biscuits on top
    • You might be able to fit two cans of biscuits on top depending on the size of your pot. I wan't able to as you can see in the photo
  7. Cook for one more hour

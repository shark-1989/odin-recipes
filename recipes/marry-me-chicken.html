Marry Me Chicken

Description

Marry Me Chicken is a dish I make at least once a week. It takes about 30 minutes to cook and can be made in one large pan, or with an additional pot if you make the chicken broth from bouillon. This recipe is great any time of year.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 tsp each of salt, pepper, Oregano, and Thyme
  • 1 tsp each of garlic powder and onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1/3 cup of sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cups of rice
  • 2-3 cups of chicken broth
    • Or 2–3 chicken bouillon cubes, which is what I normally use.
  • 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 1 1/2 Cups of shredded mozzarella cheese
    • I usually shred a block of mozzarella for a fresher taste.

Instructions

  1. (You can skip this step if you're using chicken broth.) If you are using chicken bouillon cubes, boil one cup of water per cube you plan to use. I normally use three cubes so I normally boil three cups of water. I put the water in the pot with unwrapped cubes and bring the water to a boil. I let it boil until the cubes dissolve. When done, let broth cool.
  2. Take your chicken and cut it up into small cubes. Mix the salt, pepper, Oregano, thyme, garlic powder, and onion powder in a small bowl. You can toss the chicken in the bowl to mix up the seasoning or place the seasoning into a zip-lock bag with the chicken and shake it up. Ensure the chicken is seasoned evenly. Cook the chicken on medium/ high most of way through.
  3. Once the chicken is cooked most of the way through, add your rice, sun dried tomatoes, and minced garlic to the pot. Bring to boil, cover, and reduce to a simmer. Let that cook for about 15-20 minutes or until the rice is cooked.
  4. After the rice is cooked, add 1/2 cup of heavy whipping cream and 1 cup of mozzarella cheese. Mix everything together ensuring the cheese is melted.
  5. Serve in a bowl and use the rest of the cheese to mix in each serving as desired.

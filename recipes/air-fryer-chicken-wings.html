Air Fryer Chicken Wings

Description

These chicken wings are simple to make and taste great! My kids love them, and they are easy to pair with almost any dipping sauce you can think of. Let's make some wings.

Ingredients

  • 3 Packs of Kirkland Signature Fresh Chicken Party Wings
    • Comes in a pack of six. Only three packs are needed to feed four people with some leftovers.
  • 1/3 Cup All-purpose Flour
  • Paprika
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Olive Oil
  • Air Fryer

Prepping the Wings

Prep is simple with the Kirkland party pack of wings. You don't have to cut the wings into flats (wingettes) and drums (drumettes). Simply take them out of the package and dry them off with a paper towel.

If you can't find these, get some whole chicken wings from your local grocery store. They require a little more prep because you will need to separate the wings into flats and drumettes and dispose of the tips. The tips don't have much meat on them and, in my experience, aren't very tasty. Ask me how I know.

Processing a Full Chicken Wing

  1. Place the whole chicken wing on a cutting board and locate the two joints.
  2. Using a sharp knife, cut through the joint between the drumette and the flat.
  3. Cut through the second joint to remove the wing tip.

You should now have a drumette and a flat ready for seasoning and cooking. If you need a visual reference on how to process a wing, check out this video out from WebMD.

Final Prep and Cooking

  1. Preheat your Air Fryer to 390 Degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Preheat your oven to 170 Degrees Fahrenheit or the lowest settings if you have to make the chicken in batches.
  3. Place the flour, paprika, salt, and pepper into a bowl and mix with a whisk.
  4. Lightly coat each piece with the seasoning.
  5. Once coated place the chicken in a tin pan.
  6. When the Air Fryer is preheated place approximately 10 pieces into the fryer (you can add more if you have a large fryer).
  7. Cook for 16 minutes total, flipping the chicken at 8 minutes.
  8. Place the cooked chicken in a clean tin pan and place in oven to keep warm if you're making the chicken in batches.
  9. Enjoy.

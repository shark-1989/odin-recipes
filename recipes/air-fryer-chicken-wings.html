Air Fryer Chicken Wings

These chicken wings are simple to make and taste great! Lets make some wings.

Ingredients

  • 3 Packs of Kirkland Signature Fresh Chicken Party Wings
    • Comes in a pack of 6. You only 3 packs are needed to feed four people with some left overs.
  • 1/3 Cup All-purpose Flour
  • Paprika
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Olive Oil
  • Air Fryer

Prepping the Wings

Prep is simple with the party pack of wings from Kirkland. You don't have to cut the wings into flats (wingette) and drums (drumette). All you need to do is take them out of the package and dry them off with a paper towel.

If you can't get these get some chicken wings from the local store. These take a little more prep because you have to separate or cut the wings into flats and drums. Then you will have to depsose the tips. Tips don't have much meat on them and in my experiance, are not tastey. Ask me how I know.

Processing a Full Chicken Wing

  1. Place the whole chicken wing on a cutting board and locate the two joints.
  2. sing a sharp knife, cut through the joint between the drumette and the flat.
  3. Then cut through the second joint to remove the wing tip.

You should now have a drumette and a flat ready for seasoning and cooking. If you need a visual reference on how to process a wing, check out this video out from WebMD.

Final Prep and cooking

  1. Preheat your Air Fryer to 390 Degrees Fahrenheit
  2. Preheat your oven to 170 Degrees Fahrenheit or the lowest settings if you have to make the chicken in batches.
  3. Place the flour, paprika, salt, and pepper in to a bowel and mix with a whisk
  4. Take each piece of chicken and lightly coat them with the season
  5. Once coated place the chicken in a tin pan.
  6. When the Air Fryer is preheated place approximately 10 pieces into the fryer (you can add more if you have a large fryer)
  7. Cook for 16 minutes total, flipping the chicken at 8 minutes
  8. Placed the cooked chicken in a clean tin pan and place in oven to keep warm if you're making the chicken in batches
  9. Enjoy
